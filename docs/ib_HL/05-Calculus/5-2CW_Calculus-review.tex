\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.15cm]

  \item By using the Maclaurin series for $\cos x$ and the result from

  \hfill [4]
  \item By using the Maclaurin series for $\arctan x$ and the result
  from part (b), find
  $$\lim_{x \to 0}\left(\frac{x \arctan 2x}{\sec x - 1}\right).$$
  \hfill [3]

%--- Problem 2: kinematics, v = 4 + 4t - 3t^2; max velocity, displacement, sketch, total distance [16 marks] ---
%--- Source: AA HL 2021 Nov P1 TZ0 Q10 ---
\item[2.] \makebox[\linewidth][s]{[Maximum mark: 16]\hfill {\small AA HL 2021 Nov P1 Q10}}

A particle $P$ moves along the $x$-axis. The velocity of $P$ is
$v \text{ m s}^{-1}$ at time $t$ seconds, where
$v(t) = 4 + 4t - 3t^{2}$ for $0 \leq t \leq 3$. When $t = 0$, $P$ is at
the origin $O$.

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.15cm]
  \item \begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.1cm]
    \item[(i)] Find the value of $t$ when $P$ reaches its maximum
    velocity.
    \item[(ii)] Show that the distance of $P$ from $O$ at this time is
    $\dfrac{88}{27}$ metres. \hfill [7]
  \end{enumerate}
  \item Sketch a graph of $v$ against $t$ on the axes below, clearly
  showing any points of intersection with the axes. \hfill [4]
  \item Find the total distance travelled by $P$. \hfill [5]
\end{enumerate}
