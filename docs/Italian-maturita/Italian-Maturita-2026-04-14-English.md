## For the Scientific High School Track

**Answer 4 of the following questions:**

1. Consider the following proposition:  
   *“If two solids have equal volume, then, when cut by a family of parallel planes, they determine cross-sections of equal area on each of them.”*  
   State whether the statement is true or false, and give a fully justified explanation for your answer.

2. Recalling that the side of a regular decagon inscribed in a circle is equal to the chord subtending an arc in a circle of radius \(r\), prove that  
   \[
   \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{10}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{4}.
   \]

3. Among cylindrical containers having the same surface area \(S\) (the lateral area plus the base), which one has the maximum volume?

4. State the rule of the Marquis de L’Hôpital (1661–1704) and apply it to prove that
   \[
   \lim_{x\to +\infty}\frac{x^{2008}}{2^x}=0.
   \]

5. Determine a third-degree polynomial \(P(x)\) such that:
   \[
   P(0)=P'(0)=0,\quad P(1)=0,\quad \text{and}\quad \int_0^1 P(x)\,dx=\frac{1}{12}.
   \]

6. If
   \[
   \binom{n}{1},\ \binom{n}{2},\ \binom{n}{3}
   \]
   with \(n>3\) are in arithmetic progression, what is the value of \(n\)?

7. Determine, as \(k\) varies, the number of real solutions of the equation:
   \[
   x^3-3x^2+k=0.
   \]

8. Let the function be defined by
   \[
   f(x)=\pi^x-x^{\pi}.
   \]
   Specify the domain of \(f\), and determine the sign of the first and second derivatives at the point \(x=\pi\).

9. Let
   \[
   f(x)=\frac{x^2-1}{|x-1|}.
   \]
   Does the limit
   \[
   \lim_{x\to 1} f(x)
   \]
   exist? Justify your answer.

10. According to the highway code, the “steep ascent” sign (see figure) warns drivers of a stretch of road with a slope large enough to be dangerous.  
    The slope is expressed as a percentage, and in the example it is 10%.

    If a straight road is built that, over a distance of 1.2 km, rises by 85 m in elevation, what is its inclination (in degrees)? What percentage should be shown on the sign?
