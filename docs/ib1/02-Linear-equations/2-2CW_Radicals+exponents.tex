where $a$ and $b$ are integers, or as an integer.

\begin{multicols}{2}\raggedcolumns
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.25cm]
  \item $\sqrt{12}$
  \item $\sqrt{50}$
  \item $\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{8}$
  \columnbreak
  \item $3\sqrt{5} + 4\sqrt{5}$
  \item $\sqrt{18} - \sqrt{2}$
\end{enumerate}
\end{multicols}

  \item[(h)] Hence, rationalize the denominator of $\dfrac{8}{3 - \sqrt{5}}$.
\end{enumerate}
