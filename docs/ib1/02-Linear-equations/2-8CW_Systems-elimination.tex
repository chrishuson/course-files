\item[2.]

A city studies the weekly market for bicycle helmets. The quantity is $q$
hundred helmets per week and the price is $p$ euros per helmet. Two surveys
give these results.

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.3}
\begin{tabular}{l|cc|cc}
  & \multicolumn{2}{c|}{\textbf{Demand}} & \multicolumn{2}{c}{\textbf{Supply}} \\
  & \multicolumn{2}{c|}{(shoppers buy)} & \multicolumn{2}{c}{(shops offer)} \\
  \hline
  Price $p$ (euros) & $36$ & $72$ & $33$ & $68$ \\
  Quantity $q$ (hundreds) & $16$ & $4$ & $6$ & $20$ \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Demand and supply are each modelled by a straight line $p = mq + c$.

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.1cm]
  \item Show that the demand line is $p = 84 - 3q$.
  \item Find the equation of the supply line.
  \item Write both equations in the form $aq + bp = c$. Hence, use the
  elimination method to find the equilibrium quantity and price.
  \item The city sets a minimum price of $60$ euros per helmet. Find the
  quantity demanded and the quantity supplied at this price. Hence, find
  the surplus of helmets each week.
\end{enumerate}
