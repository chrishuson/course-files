\item[1.]

The sum of the first $n$ terms of a geometric sequence is
$$S_{n} = \frac{u_{1}\left(r^{n} - 1\right)}{r - 1}
\qquad \text{or} \qquad
S_{n} = \frac{u_{1}\left(1 - r^{n}\right)}{1 - r},
\qquad r \neq 1.$$

Consider the geometric series
$$2 + 6 + 18 + 54 + \cdots$$

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.1cm]
  \item Find $S_{5}$, the sum of the first five terms, by writing out
  the five terms and adding them.
  \item Use the first formula above to find $S_{5}$. Show the values
  you substitute.
  \item Find $S_{10}$, the sum of the first ten terms.
  \item The sum of the first $n$ terms is $177\,146$. Find $n$.
\end{enumerate}

\item[2.]

starting annual salaries are close to the average for college graduates
where they live. Luca works in New York City with a starting annual salary
of \$$70\,000$. Sofia works in Italy with a starting annual salary of
\euro$30\,000$. Each of their annual salaries increases by $6\%$ every year.

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.1cm]
  \item Find Luca's annual salary in his third year.
  \item Find the total amount Luca earns in his first three years.
  \item Find the number of complete years Luca must work for his total
  earnings to first exceed \$$1\,000\,000$.
  \item Find the number of complete years Sofia must work for her total
  earnings to first exceed \euro$1\,000\,000$.
\end{enumerate}
