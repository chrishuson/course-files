\subsubsection*{2.1 Warm-up: Mental Math (no calculator)}

Work each question in your head --- no calculator, and no need to show work. Write
just the answer, with its units.

\vspace{0.15cm}

\textbf{Metric prefixes.} \quad
giga (G) = 1{,}000{,}000{,}000 \quad mega (M) = 1{,}000{,}000 \quad kilo (k) = 1000 \\[0.1cm]
centi (c) = $\dfrac{1}{100}$ \quad milli (m) = $\dfrac{1}{1000}$
\quad micro ($\mu$) = $\dfrac{1}{1{,}000{,}000}$ \\[0.1cm]
A micrometer (\si{\micro\metre}) is also called a \emph{micron}.

\vspace{0.2cm}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.55cm]

\item Fill in the blanks.
  \begin{multicols}{2}
    \begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.65cm]
      \item 2.5 km = \blank\ m
      \item 450 cm = \blank\ m
      \item 0.8 m = \blank\ cm
      \item 35 mm = \blank\ cm
      \item 1 m = \blank\ mm
      \item 7 mm = \blank\ m
    \end{enumerate}
  \end{multicols}

\item In 1791 the meter was defined so that the North Pole is exactly 10{,}000 km
  from the equator. How many meters is that? \hfill \blank[3.2cm]

\item Printer paper is 0.1 mm thick. How many sheets make a 1 cm stack? \hfill \blank

\item A phone has 256 GB of storage.
  \begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.6cm]
    \item How many megabytes (MB) is that? \hfill \blank[3.2cm]
    \item A photo takes about 4 MB. About how many photos fit? \hfill \blank[3.2cm]
  \end{enumerate}

\item A recent phone's processor runs at 4 GHz: its clock ticks 4 giga-times every
  second. Write that number of ticks in digits. \hfill \blank[4cm]

\item A human red blood cell is about 8 \si{\micro\metre} across. A phone's processor
  chip is about 1 cm across.
  \begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.6cm]
    \item How many micrometers are in 1 mm? \hfill \blank
    \item How many red blood cells, lined up edge to edge, make 1 mm? \hfill \blank
    \item How many micrometers across is the phone chip? \hfill \blank
  \end{enumerate}
