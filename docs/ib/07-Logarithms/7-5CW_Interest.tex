\subsubsection*{7.5 Classwork: Interest and Depreciation (calculator)}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.4cm]

%--- Problem 1: Savings account, growth at 4% per year ---
\item[1.] [Maximum mark: 9]

Hannah deposits \$2000 in a savings account that pays interest of $4\,\%$ per
annum, applied at the end of each year. No further deposits or withdrawals are
made.

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.4cm]
  \item Show that the value of the account at the end of the first year is
  \$2080. \hfill [1]

  \vspace{2.5cm}

  \item Find the value of the account at the end of $5$ years. Give your answer
  correct to the nearest dollar. \hfill [3]

  \vspace{4.5cm}

  \item Find the value of the account at the end of $10$ years. \hfill [2]

  \vspace{3.5cm}

  \item Find the smallest number of full years needed for the value of the
  account to first exceed \$3000. \hfill [3]

  \vspace{5cm}
\end{enumerate}

\newpage

%--- Problem 2: Car depreciation, 15% per year ---
\item[2.] [Maximum mark: 10]

Mr.\ Rossi buys a new car for \$30\,000. Each year the value of the car
decreases by $15\,\%$ of its value at the start of that year.

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.4cm]
  \item Show that the value of the car at the end of the first year is
  \$25\,500. \hfill [1]

  \vspace{2.5cm}

  \item Find the value of the car at the end of $4$ years. Give your answer
  correct to the nearest dollar. \hfill [3]

  \vspace{4.5cm}

  \item Find the smallest number of full years after which the car is worth
  less than \$10\,000. \hfill [3]

  \vspace{4.5cm}

  \item Mr.\ Rossi will sell the car when it is worth less than half its
  original value. Find the number of years after which this first occurs.
  \hfill [3]

  \vspace{4cm}
\end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
