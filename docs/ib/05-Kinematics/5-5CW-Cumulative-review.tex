Solutions
  Problem 1 — Kinematics (v(t) = 3t² − 12t + 9):
  - (a) 3t² - 12t + 9 = 0 → 3(t-1)(t-3) = 0 → t = 1, t = 3
  - (b) a(t) = v'(t) = 6t - 12
  - (c) a(2) = 12 - 12 = 0. v(2) = 12 - 24 + 9 = -3 < 0.
        Acceleration is zero, so the particle is neither speeding up nor slowing down.
  - (d) v changes sign at t = 1 and t = 3.
        ∫₀¹ v dt = [t³ - 6t² + 9t]₀¹ = 1 - 6 + 9 = 4
        ∫₁³ v dt = [t³ - 6t² + 9t]₁³ = (27-54+27) - (1-6+9) = 0 - 4 = -4
        Total distance = |4| + |-4| = 8 m

  Problem 2 — Probability (20 Bio, 18 Chem, 30 total):
  - (a) n(B∩C) = 20 + 18 - 30 = 8
  - (b) Venn: B only = 12, B∩C = 8, C only = 10
  - (c) P(B∩C) = 8/30 = 4/15
  - (d) P(B|C) = (8/30)/(18/30) = 8/18 = 4/9
  - (e) P(B) × P(C) = (20/30)(18/30) = 360/900 = 2/5 = 6/15
        P(B∩C) = 4/15 ≠ 6/15, so B and C are not independent.
  - (f) 75% of 20 Biology = 15 passed. 50% of 10 Chemistry-only = 5 passed.
        Total passed = 15 + 5 = 20.
        P(Biology | passed) = 15/20 = 3/4

  Problem 3 — Arithmetic and Geometric Sequences:
  - (a) u_20 = 5 + 19(3) = 62
  - (b) S_20 = 20/2 × (5 + 62) = 670
  - (c) u_20 = 5 × 1.1^19 = 5 × 6.1159... = 30.6 (3 s.f.)
  - (d) S_20 = 5(1.1^20 - 1)/(1.1 - 1) = 5(6.7275... - 1)/0.1 = 286 (3 s.f.)
  - (e) 5 × 1.1^(n-1) > 50 → 1.1^(n-1) > 10 → n-1 > ln10/ln1.1 = 24.16...
        → n > 25.16 → n = 26

  Problem 4 — Linear Regression:
  - (a) y = 7.54x + 31.6 (values may vary slightly by GDC)
  - (b) r ≈ 0.987
  - (c) Strong positive correlation
  - (d) y = 7.54(4.5) + 31.6 = 65.5
  - (e) Extrapolation beyond the data range; the model may not hold
