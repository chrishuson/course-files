%--- Problem 1: Mean, median, mode and right skew from EU VAT rates [8 marks] ---
%--- Source: Original (freshly authored, AA SL 4.2, 4.3). Data: Tax Foundation, 2026 EU standard VAT rates ---
\item[1.]

Most European countries charge a sales tax called \emph{VAT} (in Italy,
\emph{IVA}). The standard rate, as a percentage, is shown for twelve
countries in 2026.

{\small
\begin{center}
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{5pt}\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.1}
\begin{tabular}{lr@{\hspace{16pt}}lr@{\hspace{16pt}}lr}
Malta & 18 & Spain & 21 & Italy & 22 \\
Germany & 19 & Netherlands & 21 & Ireland & 23 \\
Cyprus & 19 & Belgium & 21 & Sweden & 25 \\
France & 20 & Austria & 20 & Hungary & 27 \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}}

\vspace{-0.15cm}

\begin{center}

  \node[anchor=west] at (28.5,0) {\footnotesize \%};

\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.06cm]
  \item Find the mean standard VAT rate.
  \item Find the median.
  \item Write down the mode.
  \item Describe the shape of the distribution shown in the dot plot, and
  justify your answer by comparing the mean and the median.
  \item One country stands out from the rest. Name it, and say in one
  sentence why you would call it an outlier.
\end{enumerate}
