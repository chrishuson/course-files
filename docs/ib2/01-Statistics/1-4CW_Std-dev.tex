\subsubsection*{1.4 Classwork: Standard Deviation}

\emph{Use your GDC. Give answers exactly, or correct to three significant
figures. Write down the values you put into the calculator.}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.15cm]

%--- Problem 1: Mean and sd from a frequency table; quartiles; values beyond one sd [9 marks] ---
%--- Source: Original (freshly authored, AA SL 4.3), GDC One-Variable Statistics with a frequency list ---
\item[1.]

A supermarket records the number of items bought by each of the $50$
customers who used the express checkout one morning.

\begin{center}
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{10pt}\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\begin{tabular}{|l|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
\hline
Number of items, $x$ & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6 \\
\hline
Frequency, $f$ & 4 & 9 & 13 & 11 & 8 & 5 \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.1cm]
  \item Find the mean number of items bought.
  \item Find the standard deviation.
  \item Write down the median, the lower quartile $Q_{1}$ and the upper
  quartile $Q_{3}$, and hence find the interquartile range.
  \item Find the number of customers who bought more than one standard
  deviation above the mean.
  \item A fifty-first customer goes through the express checkout with $20$
  items. State the effect this has on the mean and on the standard
  deviation. Give a reason for your answer.
\end{enumerate}

\item[2.]

Two machines fill bottles that are labelled $500$ millilitres. Eight
bottles are taken from each machine and the volume of each, in millilitres,
is measured.

\begin{center}
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{8pt}\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
Machine A: & $498,\; 502,\; 497,\; 503,\; 500,\; 499,\; 501,\; 500$ \\
Machine B: & $494,\; 507,\; 499,\; 505,\; 496,\; 503,\; 497,\; 499$ \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.1cm]
  \item Find the mean and the standard deviation of the volumes from
  Machine A.
  \item Find the mean and the standard deviation of the volumes from
  Machine B.
  \item The manufacturer wants every bottle to be as close to $500$\,ml as
  possible. State which machine you would choose, and justify your answer by
  referring to both statistics.
  \item Machine A is adjusted so that every bottle it fills contains
  $3$\,ml more than before. Write down the new mean and the new standard
  deviation for Machine A.
  \item Find the number of the eight Machine B bottles whose volume lies
  within one standard deviation of the mean.
\end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
